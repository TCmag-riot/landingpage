About

Host & Founder

Jae Zangiacomi

New York · First-gen Mexican-American

"I grew up watching my father build a collection of trucks and classic iron one paycheck at a time."

Throttle Culture exists because car media left someone out. As a first-generation Mexican-American, Jae grew up in a world where vehicles meant more than transportation — they were dignity, autonomy, and identity. That perspective drives every episode.

Based in New York, Jae brings a bicultural lens to storytelling about machines, the people who love them, and the culture that forms around them. The show is for everyone who has ever felt like the mainstream conversation wasn't made for them.

The Brand

Throttle Culture is an independent automotive media brand rooted in New York. We cover cars the way cars actually live in people's lives — not spec sheets and lap times, but memory, identity, and what it means to own something with a heartbeat under the hood.

Our events — Octane & Espresso — bring that ethos onto the road: convoy drives through the Hudson Valley and beyond, good coffee, and a community that doesn't need a reason to show up besides the love of the drive.

Tercer Pedal

Tercer Pedal is Throttle Culture's Spanish-language voice — a show built specifically for the bilingual and Spanish-speaking car enthusiast who has always been part of this culture, just underserved by it.

The name means "third pedal." It's insider knowledge. It's the mechanism that connects power to motion — the part only some people know how to use.
